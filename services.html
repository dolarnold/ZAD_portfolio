Our Services

Fourteen specialised offerings, grouped by where the risk actually lives — from the factory floor to the festival ground.

Group A

Workplace & Compliance

SVC-01

OSH Compliance & Management Systems

Helps organisations develop and maintain Occupational Safety & Health systems that meet legal and industry standards. Ensures workplaces operate safely and remain compliant with regulations.

SVC-04

OSH Training & Capacity Building

Provides safety training programs to equip employees with skills on hazard awareness, emergency procedures, and safe work practices.

SVC-14

Corporate & SME Safety Programmes

Provides structured safety plans tailored to businesses. Helps build a strong safety culture and reduce workplace incidents.

SVC-08

Real Estate, Facility & Property Safety

Ensures buildings and facilities are safe for occupants. Covers inspections, hazard identification, safety signage, and compliance checks.

SVC-11

Environmental Health, ESG & Sustainability

Supports organisations in meeting environmental and sustainability standards. Includes waste management, pollution prevention, and ESG reporting.

SVC-06

Fire Safety & Emergency Response

Ensures organisations are prepared for fire incidents and emergencies. Includes fire risk assessments, drills, response planning, and evacuation training.

Group B

Events & Public Safety

SVC-02

Event Safety Management & Crowd Protection

Covers planning and coordinating safety measures during events. Ensures safe crowd movement, emergency readiness, and overall incident prevention.

SVC-03

Event Data Capture & Analytics

Involves collecting real-time safety and operational data during events. Helps organisers track attendance, risks, and performance for better decision-making.

SVC-10

Film, TV & Media Production Safety

Provides safety oversight for film and media sets. Ensures cast and crew work in secure environments, especially during stunts or high-risk scenes.

SVC-05

Fleet Safety & Road Risk Management

Focuses on minimising accidents and risks related to company vehicles. Includes driver assessments, route safety planning, and vehicle monitoring.

Group C

High-Risk Industries

SVC-07

Construction & High-Risk Industry Safety

Offers specialised safety supervision, audits, and support for construction and other high-risk industries. Helps prevent accidents and ensures site compliance.

SVC-09

Agriculture, Coffee, Tea Factory & Dairy Safety

Enhances safety within agricultural processing environments. Addresses machinery risks, chemical exposure, safe handling, and worker protection.

SVC-13

PPE & Safety Equipment Supply

Supplies certified protective gear and safety equipment to ensure worker safety across various industries.

Group D

Technology & Equipment

SVC-12

Digital HSE Solutions & Automation

Delivers tech-based safety solutions such as digital reporting, automated safety alerts, and AI-driven monitoring.
